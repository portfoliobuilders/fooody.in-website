HOW TO GET THIS WEBSITE ON YOUR PC
==================================

1. Download this file from GitHub:
   Fooody.in-website-for-PC.zip

2. Unzip it (right-click → Extract All).

3. Open the folder called public_html.

4. Windows: double-click start-on-windows.bat
   Mac:     double-click start-on-mac-or-linux.command

Your browser will open the Fooody.in website.

To publish on Hostinger, upload everything inside public_html
into Hostinger’s public_html folder.
