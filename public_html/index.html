The authentic 2016 origin story

We were delivering in Kerala before the giants even landed.

Back in 2016–2017, Fooody pioneered app-based delivery in Kerala. We were in the trenches—personally painting our office walls, running custom Facebook ordering bots, printing t-shirts, and convincing restaurants to go digital.

Swiggy entered Kochi on April 3, 2018. Zomato expanded across Kerala cities like Trivandrum, Thrissur, and Alappuzha on June 27, 2018.

Fooody paused, learned, and now returns in 2026 to solve the real crisis facing Indian food businesses: margin-killing aggregator commissions and total lack of customer ownership.

Restaurant questions

The shift from orders to ownership.

What is a direct ordering platform for restaurants?

It lets guests order from your own website, branded app, QR menus, and WhatsApp — not a marketplace. You keep zero-commission economics and 100% of the customer data.

Why are restaurants in India moving off aggregators in 2026?

India’s online food delivery market is racing past $13 billion, but kitchens still lose 15–30% per order and nearly all repeat intelligence. Brands on direct channels see up to 40% higher repeat rates — and 65–70% of restaurant revenue already comes from guests who return.

Does Fooody charge commission?

No. Fooody is a transparent subscription with 0% per order, no hidden cuts, and no long-term lock-in. Cancel anytime. Your domain and user base stay yours.

How fast can a restaurant go live?

Most founding partners are designed to launch in 24 hours: branded web store, QR dine-in, and WhatsApp commerce first, then apps and POS as you scale.
